About Me

Administrative & Operations Professional with more than 14 years of hands-on experience at JGC Gulf International, supporting administrative operations, project activities, employees, vendors, and internal departments within engineering and corporate environments.

My experience combines traditional administrative and operational responsibilities with practical process improvement and data-driven workflow optimization. I have handled telecom administration, vendor invoices, employee services, utilities, accommodation, procurement coordination, payment requests, operational reporting, document management, and cross-functional coordination with Finance, management, site teams, and external service providers.

A key part of my work has been identifying repetitive manual processes and improving them through practical digital solutions. I have used Advanced Excel, Power Query, PivotTables, VBA/Macros, and Power BI to organize operational data, automate recurring tasks, improve reporting accuracy, and provide clearer visibility for decision-making.

My approach is not focused on technology for its own sake. I use technology as a practical tool to make administrative work more accurate, faster, easier to control, and easier for teams to manage.
